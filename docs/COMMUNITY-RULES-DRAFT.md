## Splitpost community rules (beta)

Splitpost is a place for creators and the people who support them. These
rules keep it safe and worth visiting. By creating an account you agree to
follow them.

**Who can join.** You must be 18 or older. One account per person, in your
own name or a name you're known by. Don't pretend to be someone else.

**Be respectful.** No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or attacks
on people for who they are.

**Keep it legal and safe.** No content that sexualizes minors, ever. No
sexual content, graphic violence, self-harm encouragement, or illegal
activity. No sharing other people's private information.

**Be honest.** No spam, scams, fake engagement, or misleading links. Don't
post content you don't have the right to share.

**No payments or promotions.** The beta has no payments, rewards or paid
promotion. Don't ask members for money or sell to them through Splitpost.

**Reporting.** If you see something that breaks these rules, use Report on
the post or profile. Reports are private; the person you report isn't told
who reported them.

**What we can do.** We may hide posts or profiles that break these rules,
and suspend or close accounts, with or without notice, especially for
serious or repeated problems. This is a beta: features may change, and the
beta may pause or end.

**Questions.** Contact: [founder to add a contact address].
