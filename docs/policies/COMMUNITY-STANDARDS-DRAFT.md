## Part A: Community Standards

### 1. Why we have standards

Splitpost is built on relationships people choose. These standards exist so that creators and members can trust each other. They govern **harmful behaviour**, not opinions. Disagreement, criticism and debate are not violations on their own.

Our early communities include faith, personal development and entrepreneurship, where people hold very different views. We protect legitimate expression and act only on conduct that breaks a clearly defined rule.

### 2. Who these standards apply to

Every member, in posts, comments, profiles, reposts, shares and referral activity. Advertisers follow the Advertising Policy, which includes these standards.

### 3. Not allowed

**3.1 Harassment and bullying.** Targeted abuse, repeated unwanted contact, degrading someone, encouraging others to pile on, or sharing content to humiliate someone.

**3.2 Hate and discrimination.** Attacking people because of a protected characteristic (for example race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or age). Discussing or criticizing ideas, beliefs or institutions is allowed.

**3.3 Threats and violence.** Credible threats, incitement to violence, glorifying recent violent attacks, or instructions for causing serious harm.

**3.4 Self-harm.** Content that encourages or instructs suicide, self-harm or eating disorders. Talking about your own experience, or about recovery, is allowed. If you are worried about someone, call or text 988 (Canada and the US).

**3.5 Nudity and sexual content.** Sexually explicit content and sexual solicitation. Splitpost is adult-only during the pilot, but it is not an adult content platform.

**3.6 Child safety.** Any content that sexualizes, endangers or exploits a minor is removed and reported to the authorities. There are no exceptions. Members must be 18 or older; do not invite or refer anyone under 18.

**3.7 Privacy violations.** Posting someone's private information (address, phone number, private photos, identity documents, financial details) without permission, or images of people in private situations.

**3.8 Impersonation.** Pretending to be another person, creator, brand or Splitpost.

**3.9 Fraud and scams.** Deceiving people for money or information, fake giveaways, phishing, or misleading investment claims.

**3.10 Spam and manipulation.** Fake accounts, buying or trading engagement, coordinated inauthentic activity, mass repetitive posting, and manipulating Connection Points or referrals (see the Rewards Rules).

**3.11 Misleading commercial activity.** Undisclosed paid promotion, false claims about products, or misrepresenting Connection Points or simulated earnings as real money or guaranteed income.

**3.12 Intellectual property.** Posting content you do not have the right to share. See the IP complaint process in the Terms of Use.

**3.13 Illegal content.** Content or activity that is illegal in Canada or Ontario, including sale of illegal goods.

**3.14 Harmful misinformation.** A narrow category: false claims that create a clear risk of serious harm, such as false emergency information or dangerous medical claims. [Review note: definition to be finalized with founder and counsel before launch.]

### 4. Commercial content by members

If you are paid, sponsored or given something of value to promote a product, say so clearly in the post (for example "Paid partnership" or "#ad"). Splitpost ads are labelled "Sponsored" and are separate from member content.

### 5. Your tools

* **Block:** stops a member from seeing your content and interacting with you, and hides theirs from you.
* **Unfollow or leave a Clique:** at any time.
* **Report:** posts, comments, profiles and ads.
* **Delete:** your own posts and comments, at any time.

## Part B: Moderation Procedures

### 6. How reporting works

1. Choose **Report** on a post, comment, profile or ad.
2. Read the safety notice: reporting is not an emergency service and is not monitored around the clock. Call 911 or your local emergency number if someone is in danger.
3. Choose a reason: Spam or scam, Harassment or bullying, Hate or discrimination, Nudity or sexual content, Violence or threats, Self-harm, Impersonation, or Something else. Add optional details (up to 500 characters).
4. We confirm we received it. The person you reported is not told who reported them.

Limits: one open report per member per item, and a limit on reports per hour and per day to prevent abuse.

> **Review note:** The current build supports reporting posts and profiles. Comments and ads must be added before this text is published.

### 7. How we review

* **Manual review:** during the pilot, a person reviews every report. There is no automated moderation.
* **Order:** safety reports (threats, self-harm) are reviewed first, then oldest first.
* **No action from reports alone:** content is never removed just because it was reported or because many people reported it.
* **Moderators** are specifically authorized. Every moderation action is recorded with a reason in an audit trail that cannot be edited.

### 8. Possible outcomes for reported content

* **Dismiss:** no violation found; nothing changes.
* **Hide:** the content is removed from every member's view and kept so it can be restored if the decision is overturned.
* **Restore:** content hidden by a moderator is returned.
* Further enforcement under Part C, if needed.

### 9. Report data retention

The reporter's details and the copy of reported content are kept while the report is open and erased 90 days after it is resolved, unless a recorded legal or safety hold applies. The reason, status, dates and audit trail are kept. See the Member Privacy Notice.

## Part C: Enforcement

### 10. Proportionate enforcement

We match the response to the seriousness, intent and frequency of the violation. Most first, minor violations lead to a warning. Serious safety, fraud or legal issues can lead to immediate action at any level.

### 11. Enforcement ladder

| Level | Action | Typical use | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warning | First minor violation; explain the rule and allow correction | Not applicable |
| 2 | Content hidden | A specific post, comment or profile detail breaks a rule | Until restored on appeal |
| 3 | Feature restriction | Repeated violations in one area (for example posting, commenting, referrals or Connection Points) | [parameter: feature restriction period] |
| 4 | Temporary suspension | Serious or repeated violations | [parameter: suspension period] |
| 5 | Removal from the pilot | Severe violations (for example child safety, credible threats, fraud) or continued violations after suspension | Permanent for the pilot |

Referral or points fraud may also lead to reversal of Connection Points, as set out in the Rewards Rules.

### 12. What we tell you

Where safe and legally permitted, when we act on your account or content we tell you:

1. what action we took,
2. which rule applies,
3. why,
4. how long it lasts,
5. what you can do to fix it,
6. how to appeal.

> **Review note:** The current build does not notify authors in the app when content is hidden. Notification (in app or by email) must exist before external participants join.

### 13. Recorded reasons

Every enforcement action records: the action, the rule, the reason, the moderator, the time and the duration. These records are used for appeals, repeat-violation tracking and reviewing our own consistency.

## Part D: Appeals

### 14. Who can appeal

Any member affected by a moderation or enforcement decision (levels 2 to 5), or a reversal of Connection Points.

### 15. Steps and timelines (proposed)

| Step | What happens | Proposed timeline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Member submits an appeal from the notice or by writing to [Support contact email], with the decision and the reasons it is wrong | Within **14 days** of being told about the decision |
| 2 | We acknowledge the appeal | Within [parameter: appeal acknowledgement time] |
| 3 | We review the decision, the evidence and the member's reasons | Decision within [parameter: appeal decision time] |
| 4a | Decision upheld: we explain why | With the decision |
| 4b | Decision corrected: we restore content or access, and reinstate any reversed points | With the decision |
| 5 | The outcome is recorded in the governance history | With the decision |

> **Review note:** The 14-day appeal window is a proposal and needs founder and counsel review.

### 16. Independence of review

Where staffing permits, a different person reviews the appeal than the person who made the decision. **During the founder-operated pilot, the same person may make the decision and review the appeal.** We tell you when this is the case.

### 17. During an appeal

The decision stays in effect while the appeal is reviewed, unless we decide otherwise. Hidden content is kept (not deleted) until the appeal is decided.

### 18. Complaints about the process

Complaints about how a decision was handled, as opposed to the decision itself, can be sent to [Support contact email]. We review them and use them to improve these procedures.

## Part E: Review of these standards

### 19. Changes

We publish changes with a new version number and date. We review these standards after the pilot, using anonymized enforcement and appeal data, including how often decisions are overturned.
