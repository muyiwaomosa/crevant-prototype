### 2. Who can use Splitpost

* During the pilot, you can only register through an **invitation** or a **valid referral link**.

* You must give us a working email address and verify it.
* If we learn that someone under 18 has registered, we close the account and delete its information, subject to the retention exceptions in the Member Privacy Notice.

### 3. Your account

* Keep your sign-in details private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
* Tell us at [Support contact email] if you think someone else has used your account.
* Do not create more than one account, share an account, or sell or transfer it.
* Choose a username and display name that follow the Community Standards (for example, no impersonation).

### 4. What Splitpost offers

* **Following:** you choose creators to follow. Following is always your choice. Splitpost never follows anyone on your behalf.
* **Clique Connection:** a closer relationship you choose with a creator you follow. These terms set no fixed limit on how many creators can be in your Clique.
* **Active Supporter:** a relationship stage for members who actively support a creator (for example, through recognized contributions or non-binding Support Intent).
* **Home:** shows content from creators you follow and creators in your Clique, newest first, with Clique content given priority.

### 5. Our distribution promise

This promise is subject to:

* safety and legal limits (for example, content hidden under the Community Standards),
* eligibility (for example, content from a suspended account),
* your own controls (for example, blocking, unfollowing or leaving a Clique).

We will not remove organic content to make room for advertising, or restrict followers' access to push creators toward paid promotion.

### 7. Blocking and reporting

### 8. Advertising

## Part B: Pilot Participation Terms

### 15. What the pilot is

The pilot is a limited, controlled test of Splitpost with a small group of adults. Its purpose is to learn whether the product works and is valuable before any wider launch.

### 16. How access works

* Registration is by invitation or valid referral link only.
* We may set and change limits on how many people can join (overall and per invitation or referral source).
* We may pause registration at any time, for any reason, including safety, capacity or review.
* An invitation or referral link does not guarantee a place.

### 17. What is simulated

During the pilot:

* creator earnings, fan pool balances, point valuations and monthly settlements are **simulated**,
* no payouts are made and nothing can be redeemed for money,
* we do not collect any payment from members,
* Support Intent is **non-binding** and no money changes hands.

Participating in the pilot does not guarantee future earnings, monetary rewards, or eligibility for any future payment program. If we ever introduce real payouts, they will come with new terms that you will need to accept separately.

### 18. Feedback and testing

We may ask for your feedback. Sending it is optional. We may use your suggestions to improve Splitpost without paying you or crediting you. The Member Privacy Notice explains how feedback is stored and when it is deleted.

### 19. Pilot data

Some activity in the pilot is test activity (for example, by the founding team). We label actual, simulated and test data separately so that results are not overstated.

### 20. End of the pilot

When the pilot ends, we will tell members what happens to their account, content and simulated records. Simulated balances have no value and do not carry over to any future program unless new terms say so.

* to operate, secure, moderate and improve Splitpost.

This licence ends when you delete the content or your account, except:

* for copies kept as moderation evidence or under a legal hold, for as long as the Member Privacy Notice says,

We do not sell your content or license it to others for their own use.

### 25. Public previews

Some content you share externally may be shown as a public preview to people without an account. Previews show only content that is eligible for preview, and stop when the content is deleted or hidden.
