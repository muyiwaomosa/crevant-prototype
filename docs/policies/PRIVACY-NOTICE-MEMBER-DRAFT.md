This notice covers the Splitpost pilot app for members. The waitlist has its own published notice. Notes for reviewers are marked **Review note** and are not part of the member-facing text.

## Part A: Member Privacy Notice

### 1. Who we are

Splitpost is operated by [Legal entity name] ("we", "us"), in Ontario, Canada. This notice explains what we collect when you use the Splitpost pilot, why, who handles it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

### 2. What we collect

**When you register**

* Your email address, and that you verified it.
* That you confirmed you are 18 or older. We do not ask for your date of birth.
* The invitation or referral link you used.
* Your agreement to the Terms of Use and this notice: the versions you saw and when you agreed.

**Your profile**

* Username, display name, and anything you add (profile picture, bio, content focus, interests).

**What you post and do**

* Posts (text, photos, video), comments, likes, saves, reposts and external shares.
* Who you follow, your Clique, and your relationship stages (Following, Clique Connection, Active Supporter), including the history of changes.
* Which posts you have seen (so Home can show you what is new) and video completion.
* Blocks you make, and reports you send.

**Activity events**

* Records of actions in the app (for example: post viewed, video completed, link shared, ad shown, ad clicked), with the time. We use them to run features, calculate Connection Points, measure the pilot and keep it safe.

**Referrals**

* When you share an attributed link: a code linking the link to you and the creator.
* When someone uses your link: that the link was opened, and whether the person registered, verified their email, confirmed they are 18 or older and followed the creator. This is how we decide whether a referral qualifies.
* Integrity signals used to detect self-referrals, duplicates and fraud. [Review note: list exact signals, for example device or network signals, before publishing.]

**Connection Points and simulated rewards**

* Points recognized, points eligible for redemption, adjustments and reversals, and simulated valuations and settlements, in a ledger.

**Commercial preferences (optional)**

* If you choose to tell us, the kinds of products or services you are interested in, and an optional purchase timeframe. These are kept **separately** from your content interests.

**Support Intent (optional)**

* If you choose to indicate willingness to support a creator: the creator, and any amount or frequency you indicate. This is **not a payment or a pledge**; no payment details are collected.

**Feedback (optional)**

* If you send feedback: your message, name, pilot role, and the part of Splitpost you were using.

**Technical information**

* Like most online services, our hosting providers keep short-lived technical logs of requests, which include network addresses and device information.

We do not collect payment details from members.

### 3. How we use it

* **To run Splitpost:** your account, Home, Discover, Cliques, posts, comments and notifications.
* **To recognize contributions:** calculate Connection Points and simulated rewards, and check referral eligibility.
* **To keep Splitpost safe:** moderation, blocking, fraud and abuse detection, security.
* **To show ads:** using only the information described in section 4.
* **To measure the pilot:** in totals and trends, to learn what works.
* **To communicate with you:** about your account, the pilot and changes to our terms.

We do not sell your personal information.

### 4. Advertising and your information

* Ads can be matched to your optional commercial preferences, your country and language, and the non-sensitive category of content you are viewing.
* **Sensitive interests are never used to target ads.** This includes, for example, religious beliefs, health, sexual orientation and political opinions, even if you follow content about them.
* We do not build lookalike audiences or retarget you across other sites.
* Advertisers receive **aggregate reports only** (for example, total impressions and clicks). We do not give advertisers your name, email, or any information that identifies you.
* You can change or remove your commercial preferences at any time in your settings.

> **Review note:** Counsel to confirm whether the content category of the post being viewed is sensitive in itself when the category is, for example, faith.

### 5. Test, simulated and actual data

We label activity as **actual** (real members doing real things), **simulated** (calculated values such as point valuations, fan pool balances and creator earnings), or **test** (activity by the founding team or test accounts). Test data is excluded from results we report about the pilot, and simulated values are always described as simulated.

* **Vercel:** website and app hosting, located in [Processing locations].

### 7. Who can see what

* Your profile, posts and comments are visible to signed-in pilot members who can see them (not to people you have blocked).
* Eligible shared posts may be shown in a public preview to people without an account: the post's text, when it was posted, its topic, and your name and username. This is on by default; you can switch it off at any time in Settings (Privacy, sharing and ads), and previews stop at once. Previews never include photos or videos, comments, likes, your other details or anything about other members. (Changed Nov 28, 2026 from opt-in; for legal review, question 27.)
* Photos and videos are stored privately and shown only through short-lived links.
* During the pilot we do not keep a separate backup copy of photos and videos. Keep your original files: if a file is lost, we may not be able to restore it. (Draft wording, Nov 26; for legal review with the rest of this notice.)
* Reports are confidential: the person you report is not told who reported them.
* Your Connection Points, Support Intent and commercial preferences are visible to you and to authorized Splitpost staff, not to other members. Creators may see aggregate Support Intent totals, not who indicated it. [Review note: confirm with founder.]

### 8. Your choices and rights

* **See or correct your information:** email [Privacy contact email]. We reply within 30 days.
* **Withdraw optional information:** remove commercial preferences, Support Intent or interests in your settings at any time.
* **Delete your account:** in your settings or by email. See Part B.
* **Complain:** if you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

### 9. Age

Splitpost is for adults 18 and older. If we learn that someone under 18 has registered, we close the account and delete its information, subject to the exceptions in Part B.

### 10. Changes

We will publish new versions with a new version number and date. If a change affects how we use information you have already given us, we will ask for your agreement again first.

## Part B: Data Retention and Deletion Procedures

### 11. Principles

* We keep information only as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it for.
* When you delete content, it disappears from Splitpost at once. Files are deleted by the server shortly after.
* When you delete your account, we delete your information except for the exceptions in section 13.
* Retention periods marked [Retention period: name] are not yet approved.

### 13. Exceptions when you delete your account

We may keep limited information after account deletion, only for these purposes and only for as long as stated:

1. **Moderation evidence:** reports and content copies involving your account, for the report retention period, or longer under a recorded legal or safety hold.
2. **Audit records:** records of administrative actions, without the content itself.
3. **Simulated ledger records:** a de-identified record of points and simulated allocations, so totals remain consistent. [Review note: confirm whether these can be fully de-identified.]
4. **Fraud and referral integrity:** a record that a registration was used for a referral, to prevent the same person generating a second reward. [Review note: define minimal form, for example a one-way code.]
5. **Legal obligations:** information we are required by law to keep, or need to establish or defend a legal claim.

### 14. How deletion is carried out

1. The member deletes the account in settings, or emails [Privacy contact email] from the account address.
2. If the request comes from another address, we may ask the member to confirm the account is theirs.
3. Content is hidden at once. Files are queued and removed by the server.
4. Records not covered by section 13 are deleted. Records covered by section 13 are kept in the minimal form described.
5. We confirm completion to the member within 30 days.
6. Every deletion run is recorded in the audit trail (counts and times, not content).

### 15. End of the pilot

At pilot close we will publish what happens to each category: which is deleted, which carries over to any next phase (only with notice), and which is kept under section 13.
