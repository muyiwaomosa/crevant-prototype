## Important: what is simulated during the pilot

**During the pilot, every financial figure you see on Splitpost is simulated.** This includes creator earnings, fan pool balances, point valuations and monthly settlements.

* No payouts are made to anyone.
* Connection Points cannot be redeemed for money, gift cards or anything else of value.
* We do not collect payment from members, for anything.
* Simulated figures are estimates that can go up or down. They are not a promise, a debt, a balance owed to you, or an investment.
* Taking part does not guarantee future earnings, monetary rewards or eligibility for any future payment program.

## Part A: Connection Points

### 1. What Connection Points are

Connection Points (CP) are Splitpost's single way of recognizing contributions that help creators and the community.

**CP are not money.** They have no guaranteed cash value, are non-transferable, and cannot be bought or sold. They are not a currency, a security, a deposit or a stored-value product. There is one points system; there are no separate currencies.

### 2. Two numbers you will see

In My Split you see two figures, both in CP:

* **Total recognized points:** all CP recognized for your contributions.
* **Redemption-eligible points:** the part of your total that would be eligible if redemption were ever enabled. During the pilot, redemption is **simulated only**.

### 3. Point table (approved initial values)

| Follow a creator | 0 | No |

The table is configurable. Changes apply **going forward only** and are announced in advance (see section 9). There are no monetary rewards, and no points, for watching ads.

Follows, Clique joins and likes earn 0 CP so that nobody has a reason to follow or like for points. Splitpost never follows anyone on your behalf.

## Part B: Referral Eligibility and Integrity Rules

### 7. How a qualified referral works

1. **Share:** you share a creator's content using your attributed Splitpost link.
2. **Discover:** a genuinely new person opens the link.
3. **Register:** they register through a valid link.
4. **Verify:** they verify their email address and confirm they are 18 or older.
5. **Connect:** they **choose** to follow the referred creator. Splitpost never follows on their behalf.
6. **Check:** Splitpost validates attribution and eligibility.
7. **Recognize:** you receive 100 redemption-eligible CP (simulated only).

### 8. Eligibility rules

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| New person | The referred person must be genuinely new to Splitpost |
| Verification | Email verified and 18+ confirmed |
| Creator relationship | They intentionally follow the referred creator |
| Attribution | Only the first eligible referral within [parameter: attribution window] gets credit |
| Duplicates | A registration can generate a referral reward only once |
| Self-referrals | Not allowed, including through second accounts or people acting for you |
| Fraud | Ineligible and reversed; may lead to enforcement |
| Direct only | You are credited only for people you refer directly. There are no rewards for referrals made by the people you referred, and no multi-level or recruitment commissions |
| No payment | Nobody pays to join, to refer, or to earn CP |
| Age | Do not refer anyone under 18 |
| Messages | Share links only with people who would want them; no spam, no bulk unsolicited messages |
| Pilot capacity | A link may stop working if registration is paused or population limits are reached; no CP are owed for referrals that could not register |

### 9. Integrity review

We may hold a referral for review before recognizing it, and investigate patterns that suggest fake accounts, coordinated activity or self-referral. Integrity checks may use limited technical signals described in the Member Privacy Notice. Confirmed abuse leads to reversal and enforcement under the Community Standards.

## Part C: Simulated fan pool and point valuation

### 10. The economic model

Of **eligible net content-associated advertising revenue**:

* 40% is allocated to the creator whose content the ad was associated with,
* 20% to one platform-wide fan pool,
* 40% to Splitpost.

General feed advertising revenue (ads not associated with a specific creator's content) goes 100% to Splitpost.

**Eligible net revenue** means revenue actually attributable to content-associated campaigns, after refunds, credits and direct third-party costs.

> **Review note:** The definition of eligible net revenue needs founder and counsel review (what counts as a direct third-party cost, timing of recognition, treatment of unpaid invoices).

### 11. Dynamic point valuation (simulated)

Each month, an **estimated** point value is calculated:

> estimated value per point = simulated fan pool funds for the period / eligible outstanding redemption-eligible points

This is an estimate. It can go up or down from month to month, for example when more points are issued or advertising revenue changes. It is not a promise, a price or a guaranteed value, and during the pilot nothing can be redeemed at it.

### 12. Monthly settlement (simulated)

Each month we run a simulated settlement: reconcile revenue, fund the simulated pool, count eligible points and calculate each member's simulated share. The result is shown for information only. No money moves.

## Part D: Creator Monetization Disclosures

### 13. What creators see

Creators can see simulated earnings attributed to their content from content-associated advertising, calculated at the 40% creator share of eligible net revenue.

### 14. What creators should know

* **Simulated only.** Creator earnings during the pilot are simulated. No payouts are made, and there is no amount owed.
* **No guarantee.** Future earnings, payout programs and eligibility are not guaranteed.
* **Attribution can change.** Figures may be corrected after reconciliation, refunds or fraud review.
* **No paid prioritization.** Creators cannot pay to rank above others in their followers' Home.
* **Your own disclosures.** If you have your own sponsorships or paid partnerships, you must disclose them in your posts (see the Community Standards).
* **Public claims.** Do not present simulated earnings as real income, in Splitpost or anywhere else.

## Part E: Non-binding Support Intent Disclosures

### 15. What Support Intent is

Support Intent lets you indicate that you would be willing to support a creator financially in the future (for example, an amount per month). It helps us learn whether direct support would be valued.

### 16. What it is not

* **Not a payment.** No money is collected and no payment details are requested.
* **Not a pledge or obligation.** You are not committing to pay anything, now or later.
* **Not revenue.** Creators do not receive it, and we report it only as non-binding interest, never as earnings.
* You can change or withdraw it at any time.

If real support payments are ever offered, they will require new terms and a separate, explicit choice by you. Your Support Intent will not be converted into a payment automatically.

## Part G: Goals, qualification and locked value (simulated)

Added Nov 27 so these rules describe what the pilot app does today (approved framework SP-PRG-001, live in the pilot app with progression switched on). **Review note:** see question 49.

### 19. Goals and qualification

* Creators and supporters each have a one-time set of goals. Meeting all of them, while in good standing, makes you **Qualified**.
* Creator goals: 20 original posts; posts in 3 different weeks within 28 days; 100 genuine followers; 5 members keeping you in their Clique; 5 returning active supporters.
* Supporter goals: 10 creators in your Clique; 30 meaningful engagements across at least 3 creators; 4 active supporting weeks within 28 days; 5 eligible external shares; 5 qualified referrals.
* Only genuine activity counts: deleted or removed content, self-activity, duplicates, reposts (for creator posts), impressions, plain scrolling and ad views do not.
* Qualification is a one-time milestone. Falling below a goal later does not remove it, and there are no recurring quotas. Advertising revenue or minimum earnings are never a goal.

### 20. Locked and unlocked simulated value

* From your first day, simulated value can build up for you: a creator's attributed share, and for supporters, the value of their redemption-eligible points in the monthly pool.
* Until you qualify, that simulated value is shown as **Locked**. Once you qualify and pass integrity review, it is shown as **Unlocked**. Statements show it as **Settled**. **Paid** is not available during the pilot.
* Locked or unlocked, it is simulated. Nothing is held for you, released, withdrawn or paid, and none of it is a balance owed to you.

### 21. Integrity holds and review

* Suspicious activity leads to review, not an assumption of guilt. A hold restricts only the affected simulated value where possible, and an account-wide hold needs a documented reason. A hold is not confiscation.
* Qualification can be revoked only by a documented, appealable decision when confirmed fraud was essential to it, never because your activity later drops. A successful appeal restores it.
* Corrections are made with new, auditable entries; history is never silently changed.
* If the pilot closes or you delete your account, simulated balances end as described in the Terms of Use and the Member Privacy Notice.
