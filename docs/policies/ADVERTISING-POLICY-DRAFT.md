# Splitpost Advertising Policy

### 1. How advertising works on Splitpost

* **Managed, not self-service.** Splitpost staff set up every campaign with the advertiser. There are no auctions or programmatic bidding.
* **Fixed price.** Each campaign has a fixed price agreed in advance.
* **Reviewed before activation.** No ad runs until Splitpost has approved it.
* **Clearly labelled.** Every ad is labelled **"Sponsored"**.
* **Two placements:**
  * **Content-associated ads** appear with a specific creator's eligible content. A share of eligible net revenue is allocated (in simulation during the pilot) to that creator and to the fan pool.
  * **General feed ads** appear in the feed and are not associated with a specific creator.
* Members are never paid, and never earn Connection Points, for viewing or interacting with ads.

### 2. Advertiser eligibility

To advertise, an advertiser must:

1. be a legitimate business or organization that can be identified,
2. be able to legally offer the advertised product or service in the places where the ad will be shown,
3. agree to this policy and to the campaign terms,
4. provide accurate contact and billing details,
5. pass any review we consider necessary (for example, confirming the advertiser's website and claims).

We may decline any advertiser or campaign.

### 3. Prohibited categories

These may not be advertised:

* illegal products, services or activities,
* tobacco, vaping and nicotine products,
* recreational drugs and drug paraphernalia,
* weapons, ammunition and explosives,
* adult or sexual products and services,
* gambling, betting, lotteries and sweepstakes [Review note: confirm whether provincially licensed operators should be restricted rather than prohibited],
* cryptocurrencies, tokens, initial coin offerings and trading platforms,
* high-risk financial products (for example payday loans, binary options, multi-level marketing, "get rich quick" schemes),
* products that make unproven health or medical claims,
* surveillance or spyware products,
* counterfeit goods and products that infringe intellectual property,
* political and issue advertising (during the pilot),
* anything that breaks the Community Standards.

### 4. Restricted categories

These may be advertised only with prior written approval and any conditions we set:

* alcohol (adult audience only, compliant with provincial rules),
* financial services and investment products (licensed providers only),
* health, wellness and supplements,
* online dating,
* employment and business opportunities,
* religious organizations and events [Review note: faith is a launch community; confirm treatment],
* education and coaching programs that promise outcomes,
* charities and fundraising.

### 5. Creative standards

Ads must:

* be truthful, and not misleading in overall impression, including in fine print, images and testimonials,
* be able to support every claim with evidence,
* show prices, conditions and material terms clearly,
* not imitate Splitpost features, member posts or system messages,
* not use shocking, sexual or exploitative content,
* not use personal attributes to make claims about the viewer (for example, "Are you struggling with debt?"),
* not suggest a creator endorses a product unless the creator has agreed and the relationship is disclosed,
* link to a working landing page that matches the ad,
* use supported formats (image and simple video), within the size and length limits we provide.

### 6. Disclosure

* Every ad is labelled "Sponsored" by Splitpost. Advertisers cannot remove or obscure the label.
* Ads associated with a creator's content are visibly separate from the creator's own content.
* If an advertiser pays a creator directly to create or promote content, the creator must disclose the relationship in the post, and the advertiser must require that disclosure.

### 7. Targeting limits

Ads may be matched only to:

* country or supported market, and language,
* members' **optional** commercial preferences (and optional purchase timeframe),
* the non-sensitive content category being viewed,
* permitted creator audience context.

Ads may **not** be targeted using:

* sensitive interests or characteristics (for example religion, health, sexual orientation, political views, ethnicity, financial hardship),
* information about members' private relationships,
* lookalike audiences, behavioural prediction or retargeting from other sites,
* any targeting of minors (all pilot members are adults).

Commercial preferences are kept separate from content interests. Following faith or health content does not make a member targetable for related ads.

### 8. Frequency

Splitpost keeps ad exposure conservative. The number and spacing of general feed ads per member are limited by [parameter: ad frequency limits]. Content-associated ads follow separate placement rules. We will not remove organic content to make room for ads.

### 9. Measurement and reporting

* We record impressions, clicks and, where applicable, video completions.
* Advertisers receive **aggregate reports only**. No names, email addresses, usernames or other information identifying a member are shared.
* We do not report figures for groups so small that a member could be identified. [parameter: minimum reporting group size]
* Conversions (for example purchases) are **reconciled manually** with information the advertiser provides. Advertisers must provide accurate conversion information and must not use Splitpost reports to identify members.
* Activity we identify as invalid or artificial is excluded from reports.

### 10. Approval and removal

1. The advertiser submits the creative, landing page and audience criteria.
2. Splitpost reviews them against this policy and records the decision and reason.
3. Approved ads are activated on the agreed dates.
4. Members can report ads. Reported ads are reviewed under the Community Standards.
5. We may pause or remove any ad, before or after activation, that breaks this policy or creates risk for members. We tell the advertiser why.
6. Advertisers can ask us to reconsider a decision by writing to [Support contact email].

### 11. Data and privacy

Advertisers do not receive personal information about members. Advertisers may not place tracking code in Splitpost or use ads to collect personal information without clear disclosure on their own landing page. Any data an advertiser collects on its own site is its own responsibility.
