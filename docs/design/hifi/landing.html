What the pilot is, plainly.

  • Adults 18+ only, through invitations, referral links or the waitlist.
  • Connection Points have no cash value and are not money.
  • Creator earnings, fan rewards and point values are simulated. Nothing is paid out.
  • No guaranteed reach, views or earnings.
  • Posts are available to all followers; reach depends on who chooses to look.
  • Features are still being built and tested.
