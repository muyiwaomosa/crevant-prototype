# SP-UX-002: value-first onboarding and member experience

Built Nov 27 for final copy and visual review. Screens are the real app,
captured on a phone (390 px) and a desktop (1280 px): `sp-ux-002-*.png` in
this folder. Nothing here changes a rule, an amount or a threshold.

## 1. Welcome (five full-screen faces)

Shown once to a brand-new account, before the name step, and any time from
Profile, Settings, **How Splitpost works**. Skip on faces 1 to 4, Back from
face 2, Continue, then **Get started** (or **Done** when reopened from
Profile). Swipe and the arrow keys move between faces; Escape closes. The
slide is switched off for members who prefer reduced motion. Illustrations are
decorative; every point is in the words.

| # | Title | Text | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Don't just post. Get your split. | On most social apps, the value you create goes to the platform. Splitpost shares it with the creators who post and the people who support them. | During the pilot, every amount is simulated and nothing is paid. |
| 2 | Your people. Your feed. | Follow the people you value and keep your favourites close in your Clique. Home shows their posts, newest first. Nobody is added for you. | |
| 3 | Your support counts. | A thoughtful comment, a save or a share helps a creator grow. Splitpost notices, and recognizes it with Connection Points. | |
| 4 | Grow. Qualify. Earn. | Simple goals show what to do next. Reach them and the split you have built unlocks, as a creator, a supporter, or both. | Pilot earnings are simulated. Points are not money. |
| 5 | Make it yours. | Post what you love, support who you value, or both, all from one account. Start by finding a few people worth following. | |

What a new member can explain afterwards (acceptance, section 7): why it is
different (face 1), how to connect (face 2), how contributions are recognized
(face 3), what My Split is and what progress needs (face 4), how to start
(face 5, then the first steps on Home).

## 2. Personalized onboarding

Unchanged setup, one account: the optional "What brings you to Splitpost?"
(Share my own work, Find creators I'll love, A bit of both) and interests. They now tailor the first steps.

## 3. First steps on Home

The existing card, tailored. Each step ticks itself off from what the server
reports; **Hide** puts the card away; it disappears when every step is done.

| Answer | Steps, in order |
|---|---|
| Share my own work | Publish your first post, Find people, Choose your Clique, Support a post you value |
| Find creators I'll love, or no answer | Find people, Follow them, Choose your Clique, Support a post you value |
| A bit of both | Find people, Follow them, Choose your Clique, Publish your first post, Support a post you value |

With an interest chosen, "Find people" reads "Start with people who post about
Food & cooking, then open a profile to read their posts." and its link,
"Discover Food & cooking", opens Discover's People view on that interest.
"Follow them" asks for three people (the approved supporter activation
definition). Heading: "Your feed is quiet" while Home is empty, otherwise
"Start here" with "2 of 4 done. A few minutes to feel at home."

## 4. My Split

Three faces: **My Split** (opens here), **My Goals**, **My Activity**.

* **My Split:** the official Splitpost symbol, "Your split so far", the total,
  As a creator and As a supporter, Unlocked and Locked, "Simulated". With
  nothing yet: "$0.00" and "Nothing yet. Your split grows as you post and as
  you support the creators you value. Start with the next step below."
  Otherwise: "Locked amounts are held until you reach your goals. Reach them
  and they unlock. During the pilot every amount is simulated and nothing is
  paid." Then **What do these mean?** (the terms and the per-journey
  breakdown) and **Next for you** (the nearest goal and one action).
* **My Goals:** each journey with a ring, "Reach every goal to unlock $X
  (simulated)", each goal with its count, a bar and one action (Create a
  post, Find a post to support, Choose your Clique, Discover creators, Share a
  post you value, Invite someone). Statuses, notices and appeals as approved.
* **My Activity:** Connection Points with "Recognition for how you help
  creators grow. 970 count your contribution and have no value. 270 came from
  qualified referrals and share in the simulated rewards pool." Then your
  contributions (newest first, reversals shown) and your invitation link.

While progression is off, the My Split face shows Connection Points and the
simulated estimate as before, and My Goals says "Goals are coming".

## 5. Recognition and milestones

* After a comment, save, repost or share, and only when the server recorded
  it: "Thoughtful comment recognized. It counts toward your supporter goals:
  22 of 30." (or "It counts toward your Connection Points." while goals are
  off). One at a time, gone after five seconds, polite to screen readers.
* A goal met since the member last looked, once, at the top of My Goals:
  "Goal complete: Keep creators in your Clique. That's 1 of your 2 supporter
  goals done." The first visit records what is already met without a burst of
  messages.

## 6. Home and account

Account settings are under Profile, Settings only (Your account is no longer
in the desktop sidebar or the phone header). Feedback stays; the pilot line
stays in the desktop sidebar.

## First-use tips

* My Split: "This is your split: what you've accumulated so far, simulated
  during the pilot. Locked amounts unlock when you reach your goals under My
  Goals."
* Clique: "Your Clique is the creators you want closest. Their new posts come
  first on Home, and supporting them is recognized in My Split. Add or remove
  people at any time."
