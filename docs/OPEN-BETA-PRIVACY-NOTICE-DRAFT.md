## Splitpost beta privacy notice

This notice explains what we collect when you create a Splitpost beta account
at app.splitpost.ca, why, where it's stored, how long we keep it, and your
choices. Splitpost is operated by [legal name], [city, province], Canada.
Contact: [privacy contact email].

**What we collect**

* **Your account:** your email address and a password (stored only as a
  secure hash by our authentication provider), when you created your account,
  when you confirmed your email, and when you last signed in.
* **Your confirmation that you're 18 or older** and that you agree to the
  community rules.
* **What you choose to share:** your username, display name, optional profile
  details and interests, your posts and comments, who you follow, likes, and
  who you add to your Inner Clique. Other members can see your profile and
  posts.
* **Feedback you send us**, and reports you make about content (kept private
  from the person reported).
* **Technical information:** your IP address and browser details, which our
  providers process to deliver the service and to protect it (for example,
  rate limits and the security check).

We don't collect payment information: the beta has no payments or rewards.
Photo posting is off during the beta.

**Why we use it**

To run your account and the service, keep it safe (preventing spam, abuse and
automated sign-ups, and enforcing the community rules), send you account
emails (confirmation and password reset), and improve Splitpost using your
feedback. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

**Who processes it, and where**

* **Supabase** stores your account and content, in **Canada** (Canada Central).
* **Resend** sends account emails; your email address passes through it in
  the **United States**.
* **Cloudflare Turnstile** checks that sign-ups and sign-ins come from people,
  using technical information from your browser.
* **Vercel** serves the website.

These providers may process information in other countries, under their own
privacy and security commitments.

**How long we keep it**

While your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete
your account and content within [30] days, except where we must keep
something for safety or legal reasons (for example, a report under
investigation). Moderation records are kept as described in [the moderation
retention: 90 days after a report is resolved, unless held].

**Your choices**

You can update your profile at any time. You can ask to see, correct or
delete your information by emailing [privacy contact email]. If you're not
satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy
Commissioner of Canada.

**Changes**

If we change this notice, we'll update the version and date, and tell you in
the app before the change applies to you.

Version [1.0], effective [date].
