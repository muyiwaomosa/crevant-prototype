# Retired landing page photos

These photos were used on the earlier Splitpost homepage. The revised design
(2026-11-18) uses a new, founder-reviewed shortlist instead (see
`src/public/landing/photos.ts`); the sample avatar and the founder's photo were
restored to `public/media/landing/`, and the filmmaker photo is back in use (Future mosaic, pending approval). The three below are kept here, outside
`public/`, as alternatives: they stay in the repository but are not shipped.

All stock photos are licensed from Pexels (Pexels License,
https://www.pexels.com/license/), cropped, resized, converted to WebP and
stripped of metadata. They show models, never Splitpost members.
